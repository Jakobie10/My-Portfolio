# About Me

## Hello, I'm James Arowosoye

I am an Information Technology professional with a passion for using technology to solve business problems and improve organizational efficiency. My journey into IT has been driven by curiosity, continuous learning, and a desire to create practical solutions that make a meaningful impact.

Before transitioning into Information Technology, I gained several years of experience in logistics, inventory management, and operations. Those experiences strengthened my leadership, communication, analytical thinking, and problem-solving skills. Today, I combine that operational background with technical expertise to bridge the gap between business needs and technology solutions.

I am currently completing a Bachelor of Applied Science in Information Technology at Ensign College, where I have developed hands-on experience in cloud computing, cybersecurity, Linux administration, Windows Server, networking, scripting, business intelligence, database management, and technical support. Through academic projects, professional work, and self-directed learning, I continue expanding my knowledge while applying industry best practices.

Professionally, I have served as a Technical Support Engineer, Business Intelligence Analyst, Web Developer, and Co-Founder of a facilities management company. These roles have allowed me to support users, develop technical solutions, analyze business data, manage projects, and collaborate with diverse teams.

My goal is to build secure, reliable, and scalable technology solutions that help organizations grow while maintaining the highest standards of professionalism, integrity, and continuous improvement.
