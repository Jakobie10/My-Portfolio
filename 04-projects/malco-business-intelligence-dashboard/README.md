## Executive Summary

This project presents a Business Intelligence solution developed to improve operational visibility and decision-making at MALCO HAULAGE. Leveraging operational data from fleet management, inventory control, fuel usage, and maintenance activities, the solution transforms raw data into meaningful insights through interactive dashboards and visual reports.

Using Microsoft Power BI, with knowledge transferable to Tableau, I designed dashboards that allow managers to monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), identify operational trends, and evaluate business performance in real time. The solution reduces dependence on manual spreadsheet reporting while improving the speed and accuracy of management decisions.

This project demonstrates my ability to collect, analyze, model, and visualize business data using industry-standard Business Intelligence tools to support operational efficiency and strategic planning.

---

## Business Challenge

As a logistics company, MALCO HAULAGE manages a significant volume of operational data daily, including fleet activities, spare parts inventory, diesel (AGO) consumption, lubricant usage, maintenance records, supplier transactions, and stock movements. While this information is essential for effective decision-making, it often exists in separate spreadsheets and manual records, making analysis time-consuming and limiting management's ability to identify trends quickly.

The organization required a centralized reporting solution capable of consolidating operational data into a single, interactive dashboard. Management needed real-time visibility into key performance indicators to improve inventory control, monitor fuel consumption, track maintenance activities, and support more informed operational decisions.

The proposed Business Intelligence solution addresses these challenges by transforming raw operational data into clear, interactive visualizations that enhance transparency, improve reporting efficiency, and support data-driven decision-making across the organization.

#### Architecture Overview

Operational data from fleet management, inventory, fuel usage, maintenance, procurement, and stock movement is collected and prepared for analysis. Using Power Query, the data is cleaned and transformed before being modeled within Power BI. The dashboard design can also be implemented in Tableau, demonstrating flexibility across Business Intelligence platforms.

Interactive dashboards present key performance indicators, trends, and performance metrics that enable management to monitor operations, identify opportunities for improvement, and make informed business decisions.
